Title: ISO 20776-2:2021 Clinical laboratory testing and in vitro diagnostic test systems -
 Susceptibility testing of infectious agents and evaluation of performance of antimicrobial
 susceptibility test devices - Part 2: Evaluation of performance of antimicrobial susceptibility
 test devices against reference broth micro-dilution
Edition 2. Publication date 2021-12. Status: Published. Previous edition ISO 20776-2:2007: Withdrawn.
Scope (abstract): establishes acceptable performance criteria for AST devices used to determine
 MICs of bacteria to antimicrobial agents in medical laboratories; specifies requirements for AST
 devices and procedures for assessing performance; defines how a performance evaluation is conducted.
